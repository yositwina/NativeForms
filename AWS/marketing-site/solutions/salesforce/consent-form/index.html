The problem

Getting a signature is easy. Getting it onto the record isn't.

Consent forms, waivers, and simple agreements are everywhere — but the signed copy rarely ends up clean, personalized, and attached to the right Salesforce record.

01

Print, sign, scan, email

The classic loop: send a PDF, hope it comes back signed, then someone files the scan by hand — if it lands in Salesforce at all.

02

DocuSign is overkill (and priced for it)

For a one-page consent or waiver, per-envelope e-sign pricing and a separate platform are a lot — and it still needs syncing back to Salesforce.

03

Nothing is personalized

A generic PDF can't say "Dear Dana, regarding your Volunteer agreement dated…" without someone editing each copy by hand.

04

No proof of who agreed

An emailed signature with no identity check is weak evidence. You can't easily show the right person agreed to this exact text.

Good to know

A practical record — check your legal requirements.

TwinaForms preserves the exact document the person agreed to, their drawn signature, and (with User Verification) a verified email and timestamp, in a signed PDF on the Salesforce record. Whether that satisfies the rules for a legally binding electronic signature depends on your jurisdiction and the type of agreement, so please consult your legal team about requirements such as ESIGN, UETA, or eIDAS for your use case.

FAQ

Common questions about Salesforce consent forms.

What is a Salesforce consent form with e-signature?

It is a public TwinaForms form that shows a consent or agreement document personalized with Salesforce data, lets the person sign it on screen, and attaches a signed PDF back to a Salesforce record. The document is built with a rich-text merge editor, so you write the text once and drop in Salesforce fields like {{Prefill_Contact.LastName}} that fill in per recipient.

How does the merge document personalize the agreement?

In the Designer you write the consent text in a rich-text editor and insert merge tokens that reference Salesforce fields, for example "Dear {{Prefill_Contact.LastName}}". When a specific person opens their link, prefill loads their Salesforce data and the tokens are replaced with their real values, so each signer reads an agreement personalized to them.

What happens on the Salesforce record when someone signs?

On submit, TwinaForms generates a signed PDF that contains the merged document text and the drawn signature, and attaches it to the Salesforce object you choose (standard or custom). The submission also records the signer's details, and when User Verification is on, the verified email and the time of signing are captured too — so you have a practical record of who agreed to what and when.

Is this a DocuSign alternative?

For simple, single-signer agreements and consent it can replace DocuSign: a personalized document, an on-screen signature, and a signed PDF on the Salesforce record, with no per-envelope fee and no separate platform. It is intentionally basic compared to DocuSign and does not do multi-party signing routing or a certified enterprise audit trail. For complex or highly regulated signing workflows, a dedicated e-signature platform is still the right tool.

Is the e-signature legally binding?

TwinaForms captures a drawn signature, the merged document the person agreed to, and (with User Verification) a verified email and timestamp, all preserved in a signed PDF on the Salesforce record. Whether that meets the legal requirements for a binding electronic signature depends on your jurisdiction and use case, so consult your legal team about requirements such as ESIGN, UETA, or eIDAS for your specific agreements.

Can I confirm who actually signed?

Yes. Turn on User Verification so the signer must enter a one-time code sent to the email on their Salesforce Contact before the document opens. The signature is then tied to a verified identity, with no Salesforce login or per-user license for the signer.

Which Salesforce object does the signed PDF attach to?

Any standard or custom object you choose in the form's submit configuration. The signed PDF is attached as a Salesforce File on that record, so a consent can live on the Contact, a waiver on a custom Agreement object, a release on a Case, and so on.
